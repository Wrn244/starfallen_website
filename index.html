About Me

William Nelson

Hi, I'm William Nelson. I'm a Marine Corps Operation Inherent Resolve veteran, a lifelong gamer, music creator, storyteller, and someone who has spent the last year rebuilding myself from the ground up. After losing over 100 pounds, I've made fitness a major part of my life. I'm in the gym several times a week, running on my off days, and constantly pushing myself to become stronger, healthier, and more disciplined.

Creatively, I'm drawn to worlds that feel larger than life. I love video games, anime-inspired storytelling, dark fantasy, power fantasy, concerts, reading, and building original characters and lore. A lot of my current creative work centers around Will Starfallen, a character born from ideas of survival, rebirth, loss, power, and finding meaning through endless struggle.

I create AI-assisted music and fiction projects, blending my own ideas with tools that help bring them to life. My work often leans into emotional, cinematic, and heavy themes: fallen heroes, fractured halos, void-born power, ancient magic, looping timelines, and characters who refuse to break even when the world demands it.

Outside of creative work, I value discipline, loyalty, growth, and honesty. I've been through major personal changes, and I'm still building toward the next version of myself every day. This site is where I share my projects, stories, music, ideas, and the worlds I'm creating one piece at a time.

History

History of Will Starfallen

Will Starfallen's history is not a straight line. It is a chain of worlds, deaths, rebirths, broken prophecies, and choices that fate itself could never fully control. Born from the Void and marked by the Starfall, Will became a wanderer between realities - a guardian shaped by loss, ancient power, and the refusal to let others be swallowed by destiny.

01

The Starfall

Will's beginning was not birth in the ordinary sense. He emerged from a falling star, a fragment of impossible light cast down from the high constellations into a world that had no place for him. To some, his arrival was an omen. To others, it was a wound in the sky. But to Will, it was the first breath of a life already outside the laws of fate.

02

The Void-Born Anomaly

As Will grew, it became clear that he was not bound to destiny like others. Prophecies bent around him. Powers meant to command souls could not claim him. Fate could see the paths of kings, monsters, gods, and heroes, but Will remained a blank space in her design. He was not chosen. He was not written. He simply endured.

03

The Endless Loops

Death did not release Will. Again and again, he was thrown back into the cycle, carrying memories no one else could keep. Each loop carved away another piece of who he used to be, but also sharpened the one thing reality could not break: his will. He learned worlds by losing them, learned people by grieving them, and learned hope by rebuilding it from nothing.

04

The Ancient Pact

In a forgotten timeline, deep within a hidden marsh untouched by prophecy, Will found the primordial toads - ancient beings who channeled natural energy older than mortal magic. He did not simply learn from them. He forced a union between Void and Nature, binding two powers that were never meant to coexist. It should have destroyed him. Instead, it made him something new.

05

The Toad Sage of the Void

Through the pact, Will became the Toad Sage of the Void, a living contradiction of emptiness and life. His power no longer came from fate, gods, bloodlines, or prophecy. It came from balance, instinct, breathing, nature, and unbreakable will. In battle, he moves like a survivor of countless endings - calm, brutal, protective, and impossible to fully predict.

06

The Road Between Worlds

Now Will walks between worlds as a guardian of broken timelines and people caught in wars they never asked for. He carries old light, ancient grief, and the memory of every failure that taught him how to protect what remains. He is not searching for glory. He is searching for a way to break the cycle - and for the people worth saving when the stars begin to fall again.

Outcome Control

Will's strength is not based on brute force alone. His true power lies in control over timing, consequence, inevitability, and the narrowing of possible outcomes.

Title

The Elder Sage of the Void

As the Elder Sage of the Void, Will blends Void authority with Sage discipline, turning stillness, patience, and impossible presence into weapons.

Battle Logic

Option Removal

Will does not fight by chasing victory. He strips away enemy choices until only one path remains: the ending he has already chosen.

Fate Law

Inevitable End

A fundamental law that allows Will to enforce a true ending upon anything he strikes. It bypasses regeneration, immortality, and durability. The closer a target is to escaping death, the stronger this law becomes.

Fate Law

Eternal Return

Upon death, Will resets to a prior state in reality while retaining memory. This grants infinite adaptation and allows him to refine strategies across loops until victory becomes certain.

Fate Law

Bound Existence

Will can perceive and manipulate the threads connecting cause and effect. By altering these threads, he can force actions to fail or succeed regardless of conventional logic.

Toad Sage Breathing

Still Pond Strike

Absolute stillness followed by an instantaneous strike. It requires complete suppression of movement and intent, making opponents unable to perceive the moment of attack. Inside Primordial Mire, it becomes unavoidable.

Toad Sage Breathing

Leaping Torrent

Toad Sage Breathing

Toad Sage Breathing

Bond

The Kitsune Bond

Kitsune is more than a companion beside Will Starfallen. She is his mirror, his counterweight, and one of the few presences capable of reaching the parts of him that endless loops tried to bury. Where Will carries the silence of the Void, Kitsune carries flame, instinct, mischief, and fierce devotion.

Their bond is not simple romance, ownership, or prophecy. It is a soul-deep connection forged through trust, survival, and the strange gravity between two beings who were never meant to fit cleanly into fate's design. Kitsune challenges Will when he withdraws too far into himself, steadies him when the loops become too heavy, and reminds him that protection is not the same as isolation.

In battle, the Kitsune Bond becomes a force of balance. Will brings restraint, inevitability, and Void-born control. Kitsune brings speed, fire, illusion, emotional truth, and wild magic. Together, they do not simply fight side by side - they create openings neither could reach alone.
